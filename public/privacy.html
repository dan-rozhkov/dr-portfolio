Privacy

This portfolio counts views of published pages and case studies, case completions, and CV and contact-link clicks. Only a fixed page path, language, event type, case label and contact channel (email or LinkedIn) can enter analytics.

The site sends these events through its Cloudflare Worker to the existing PostHog EU project. Every event uses the same site-wide aggregate label. Analytics cannot distinguish visitors or sessions. It does not use cookies, browser-storage identifiers, fingerprinting, session recording, link targets, form contents, referrers, or URL query strings and fragments. The PostHog browser SDK is not loaded. Old PostHog storage is removed where browser permissions allow.

Cloudflare receives ordinary connection data, including your IP address, to deliver the site and receive event requests. The Worker does not forward your IP address, cookies, User-Agent or Referer to PostHog. PostHog receives the server connection and event time. These providers may process infrastructure logs under their own policies. This page does not promise that hosting infrastructure processes no personal data.

PostHog stores individual sanitized events for aggregate counts. This is not a database containing only precomputed totals. Earlier consent-based analytics data may still exist in the project. This change does not delete historical records. The site respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control for new analytics requests.

No cookies does not automatically mean that consent is unnecessary. Applicable requirements depend on jurisdiction and provider settings. External email and LinkedIn destinations have their own privacy practices.

See Cloudflare privacy and PostHog privacy. Questions: contact Danil.

The portfolio also loads its General Sans fonts from Fontshare. The font provider receives ordinary connection data when your browser requests these fonts.
