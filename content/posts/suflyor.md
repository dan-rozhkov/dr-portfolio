## Idea

:::logline
A prompter stays out of sight and gives you the line you need.
:::

Suflyor does the same job on a call, so I made the brand a character. It's a speech bubble with one eye open and one in a wink: it follows the talk and it's on your side.

## Logo

![Suflyor logo: a red speech bubble with a wink, next to the wordmark on cream](/images/writing/suflyor/logo-cream.webp "full")

I kept the mark to one red box and white eyes pushed into the top right corner, so the bubble reads first and the face second. The pupils are cut out in the box color, which keeps the mark simple at any size.

The wordmark is drawn letter by letter: heavy, wide and round. I balanced the spacing by the white between the letters, then tuned the tricky pairs by eye.

![Suflyor logo on a burgundy background](/images/writing/suflyor/logo-burgundy.webp "full")

## Icon

![The Suflyor app icon next to the same icon on an iPhone Home Screen](/images/writing/suflyor/icon-home-screen-v2.webp "full")

The mark sits on warm beige, so it stands apart on the iPhone Home Screen.

At 16 px brows turn into noise, so the favicon drops them, smooths the edge and enlarges the eyes.

![Simplified Suflyor favicon with larger eyes and no brows](/images/writing/suflyor/favicon-16x10.webp)

## Motion

:::logline
The mark is a character, so it acts.
:::

The bubble holds a pose, then snaps to the next one: it winks, looks around, jumps in surprise and turns its tail to the other side. The edge wobbles slightly, so it feels drawn by hand.

![The Suflyor mark winks, looks around, jumps in surprise and turns its tail](/images/writing/suflyor/card.mp4 "full")

## App

:::logline
It keeps up with the call, so you can stay in it.
:::

Pick the two languages, tap Start and the translation appears a moment after the speech, with the sentence in progress in bold. When you need an answer, the app suggests one to say out loud.

![Suflyor on an iPhone: the app opens, a call in English is translated into Spanish live, and a suggested reply appears](/images/writing/suflyor/app-call-es.mp4 "full")

During the call, the live translation and transcript stay next to suggested replies, chapters and notes, so you can answer without losing the thread.

![A hand holding a tablet with Suflyor in landscape: a live Italian to English call transcript on the left, suggested replies, call chapters and notes on the right](/images/writing/suflyor/app-tablet.jpg "full")

## Desktop

:::logline
The call ends, the work goes on.
:::

On the desktop the call gets the whole window. New meeting opens it in recording mode, and the Stop button grows into a black bar with the timer. The translation runs in the middle with the original Italian under the sentence in progress. When someone asks a question, suggested replies open in the side column above the chapters and notes.

![Suflyor on desktop: New meeting starts a call, the Stop button opens into a black bar with the timer, Italian speech arrives as English lines with the original under the current sentence, a question brings three suggested replies, and Stop opens the meeting notes](/images/writing/suflyor/desktop-live.mp4 "full")

After the call, the meeting turns into notes. Action items with owners come first, then a short overview by topic. The people from the call and your recent conversations with them sit on the right, and the field at the bottom answers questions about this meeting.

![Suflyor meeting notes on desktop: action items with owners, a done item crossed out, an overview below, people and recent conversations on the right](/images/writing/suflyor/desktop-notes.webp "full")

Ask Suflyor searches all your meetings at once. Type @ to point it at a person, a company or a meeting, or connect Slack, Notion, Gmail, Calendar and Zoom for fuller answers.

![Ask Suflyor on desktop: a large question field, connected apps under it and three example questions in one block](/images/writing/suflyor/desktop-ask.webp "full")
